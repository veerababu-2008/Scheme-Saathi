How SchemeSaathi Works

SchemeSaathi AI bridges the gap between Indian citizens and complex social welfare guidelines through transparent, deterministic rules and conversational AI assistance.

1. Deterministic Rule Engine

Unlike opaque AI models that hallucinate eligibility, SchemeSaathi evaluates citizen data against official eligibility rules using boolean logic trees (AND, OR, NOT) with precise operators (greater than, less than, in list).

GREEN: Eligible AMBER: Missing Info / Discretionary RED: Disqualified

2. RAG-Powered Conversational AI

Saathi AI retrieves knowledge chunks from official government gazettes, department portals, and verified scheme guidelines. When Saathi answers, every claim is anchored with a source citation.

3. Privacy First — No Sensitive Identity Storage

SchemeSaathi does not collect or store Aadhaar numbers, PAN cards, or biometric data. We only process non-sensitive demographic parameters (age, gender, state, land holding, income slab) necessary to evaluate welfare schemes.

4. Document Readiness Checklist

Before standing in lines at MeeSeva or CSC centres, citizens can review and check off required certificates with local language names (Telugu, Hindi, English).
